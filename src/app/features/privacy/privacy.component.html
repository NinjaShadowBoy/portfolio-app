This Privacy Policy explains what information this portfolio website collects, why it is collected, and how it is used. By using this site you agree to the practices described below.

Information I collect

  • Contact details - when you use the contact form, your name, email address, and message are stored so I can read and reply to you.
  • Account details - if you sign in with Google or GitHub, I receive your name, email address, and profile picture from that provider to create your account.
  • Ratings and comments - any rating or review you post on a project is stored and shown publicly alongside your display name.
  • Session data - a login token is kept in your browser so you stay signed in. It never leaves your device except to authenticate requests to the backend.

How I use your information

Your information is used only to operate this site: to respond to messages, to authenticate you, and to display the ratings and comments you choose to post. I do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Third-party services

Signing in relies on Google and GitHub, and uploaded images are hosted on Cloudinary. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask me to access, correct, or delete the personal data associated with your account or your messages at any time. Signing out clears the login token from your browser.
